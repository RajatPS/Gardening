Plant Nursery & Garden Management Platform

Project Overview

A full-stack Plant Nursery and Garden Management Platform where customers can:

- Purchase plants, pots, pot stands, fertilizers, medicines, and gardening tools.
- Book gardening services.
- Subscribe to monthly plant maintenance plans.
- Use AI-powered plant assistance.
- Detect plant diseases using images.
- Experience products in their home using AR.
- Receive watering and maintenance reminders.
- Communicate directly with gardening experts.
- Access multilingual support with text-to-speech functionality.

The platform will include:

- Web Application (Laravel + Tailwind CSS)
- Mobile Application (Flutter)
- Shared Laravel REST API Backend
